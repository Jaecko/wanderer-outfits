# Wanderer Outfits

The right piece on at the right moment, and off again after: your Carrot on a Stick on a mount, your fishing hat with the pole, an Argent Dawn trinket in the zones you choose, your dungeon gear inside. Whole outfits worn in one click too. Made for World of Warcraft Forever.
An extension of [Wanderer](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer): it needs Wanderer 1.4 or later. Optional: Wanderer Bags.

- Outfits of only a few pieces: in an outfit's card, a click on a piece leaves it aside. "For a moment" makes one from what you wear with the pieces that moment usually wants.
- Outfits that go on by themselves: tie one to a moment (on a mount, fishing, chosen zones, a dungeon or a raid, a town or an inn); what it changed comes back after, a piece you changed yourself meanwhile stays yours. Never in a fight; Shift held lets a moment pass.
- Suggestions when you carry a piece made for a moment; a notice when one is still worn entering a dungeon or at a ready check.
- Undo the last change, exactly; two copies of a ring told apart by enchant, gems and suffix; upgrades offered to your outfits.
- Whole outfits kept under a name and worn in one click (the game's equipment manager keeps them when the client has one); a button on the character sheet, a chooser at the mouse.
- The fishing pole in hand on a word, your weapons back after (two settings, off).
- Outfit pieces named in tooltips, never sold as junk, never put up for auction.
- Opened from Wanderer's menu, the character sheet, a key of yours or /woutfits. Settings in Wanderer's options.
