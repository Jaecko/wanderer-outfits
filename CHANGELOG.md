# Wanderer Outfits 1.2.11

### Changed
- With a controller, the button back to Wanderer's sheet above the game's one is reached with the triggers.
- With the game's look (a choice under Wanderer's Appearance): the character sheet (its stat groups and lines, its slots) drawn with the game's own parts, its lit and chosen rows, its dividers and colors. Needs Wanderer 1.4.89.

### Fixed
- Outfit names stop at the game's own 16 letters, so a renamed outfit never loses its settings.
