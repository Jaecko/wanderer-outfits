# Wanderer Outfits 1.2.10

### New
- A character sheet of its own, opened by the character key: your character large in the middle (drag to turn it, the wheel brings it a little closer), your pieces in two columns and your weapons below, each with its name in its quality color and, under it, its item level beside the square, its enchant in green and a word in red when it is broken or nearly, on a dark band fading toward your character. An empty place shows only its outline.
- Your character stands in a soft warm light between two darker sides, a little turned at first. It comes in slowly as the sheet opens, and a piece you change appears the same way.
- Your name, level, class, guild and item level at the top; beside, your characteristics as the game groups them (its own explanation under the mouse), your outfits and your titles.
- A piece works as in the game: click or drag to take it, drop an item on it (or on your character) to put it on. Right click, or Alt under the mouse: the pieces of your bags that fit there, each with its item level, and the one worn put away in your bags. The card follows your bags as they change.
- The game's reputation, currency and other pages are one click away at the foot; "Game window" gives the game's whole sheet back for this character, and a link above it brings Wanderer's back. A setting keeps the game's sheet.

### Changed
- A piece of an outfit in your bank is no longer faded: its bank mark is larger.
- Deleting an outfit says Delete on the button and in the question.
- The fight message says the change waits for the end of the fight.

### Fixed
- Saving an outfit again keeps the places it leaves alone (set in the game's equipment manager): they are no longer swallowed into it.
- Shift+click on a piece in an outfit card links it, Ctrl+click tries it on, without changing the outfit.
- With a controller, the character sheet takes the controller as it opens (it waited to be chosen).
