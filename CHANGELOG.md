# Wanderer Outfits 1.2.0

First release, an extension of Wanderer (needs Wanderer 1.4 or later). Optional: Wanderer Bags.

### New
- Outfits kept under a name and worn in one click: your dungeon gear, your travel clothes, your fishing trip. The game's own equipment manager keeps them when the client has one; else Wanderer keeps them for each character and dresses you piece by piece.
- An outfit can take only a few pieces: a riding trinket and gloves, a fishing hat, nothing else. In its card, a click on a piece leaves it aside or takes it again.
- Outfits that go on by themselves: tie one to a moment (fishing, on a mount, chosen zones, a dungeon or a raid, a town or an inn) and it goes on when the moment comes. What it changed comes back after; a piece you changed yourself meanwhile stays yours. Said once, the first time, so you know why.
- For a moment: an outfit made from what you wear with the pieces that moment usually wants, its card open to keep only the right ones.
- Suggestions: you carry a piece made for a moment (Carrot on a Stick, Lucky Fishing Hat, Nat Pagle's boots, the Argent Dawn's trinkets) and no outfit takes it yet: once for each piece, a notice offers the outfit that puts it on by itself. One click makes it, and what you wore is given back.
- Still worn: entering a dungeon, or at a ready check, a piece made for a moment still on you is said; one click undoes the change.
- Undo the last change: exactly what you wore before the last change of gear (an outfit, a moment, the pole) put back; again, redone. A button in the window, an entry in the chooser, a key of your own, /woutfits undo.
- The very copy: two rings or two trinkets of the same name told apart by their enchant, gems and suffix.
- An upgrade put on by hand in place of an outfit's piece: a notice offers to put it in those outfits too.
- Read at a glance: each outfit says worn, pieces to put on, pieces in the bank or pieces missing, with a strip of its pieces from head to ranged.
- An Outfits button on the character sheet; a key of your own opens a chooser at the mouse; keys 1 to 9 over the window.
- The fishing pole in hand on a word (a key, the window's button, /woutfits pole), your weapons remembered and back on the next press. Two options, off: the pole in hand when you cast with Wanderer's double click at a spot you have fished from, with your weapons back once you are done; your weapons back when you mount.
- Outfit pieces protected: named in item tooltips, never sold as junk, never put up for auction by Wanderer Auction. With Wanderer Bags: a section of their own, and their pieces taken from the bank in one click at the banker.
- Fight safe: nothing changes during a fight, it is done when the fight ends. A missing piece never stops the rest; one short line names what stayed in the bank or was not found. Shift held lets a moment pass.
- Steps aside for ItemRack and for Naowh Forever's automatic gear sets, so two addons never dress you at the same moment.
- Outfits and settings per character; ten languages.
